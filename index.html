    Method

    1. National figures: Real Estate:UK (formerly the British Property Federation) quarterly Build-to-Rent reports, produced with Savills using Molior and Glenigan data. Each quarter is shown as first published.
    2. Scheme register: one row per scheme phase, drafted by AI from public sources (planning records, operator, contractor and press pages). Every row carries its sources and the date of the newest one.
    3. Definitions: minimum 20 homes; multifamily build-to-rent, co-living and single-family housing counted separately; pipeline includes pre-planning.
    4. Rows stay Unverified until a colleague has checked them against their sources. Rows whose notes say VERIFY need checking first.
